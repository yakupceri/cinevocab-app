# Chrome Web Store listing (English, default language)

## Name
CineVocab

## Short description (max 132 chars, also in _locales/en)
Translate Prime Video, YouTube & Udemy subtitles word by word. Any language pair, word lists, quizzes and live duels.

## Detailed description
Learn the words you actually meet while watching. CineVocab shows the translation of every subtitle word as you watch, saves the words for you, and turns them into quick quizzes and live duels against your friends.

HOW IT WORKS
- Pick your language pair in the popup and turn the extension on.
- Watch on Prime Video, YouTube or Udemy. Every subtitle word appears with its translation, and the whole sentence is translated below it.
- Your words are saved automatically and grouped by platform, show and episode.
- Practice them on the CineVocab website, or challenge friends in a live duel.

FEATURES
- Works with any language pair (for example French to English, Turkish to English)
- On/off switch, and a language guard that pauses translation when the subtitles are not in the language you chose
- Subtitle box you can move to any corner, resize and make more or less transparent
- Word lists by platform, show and episode, with thumbnails
- Quick quizzes with levels, streaks, daily goals and combos, plus survival and speed modes
- Live duels: create a room, share the 4-digit code, race your friends. After each duel you see the words you missed and can practice them right away
- Leaderboards (people and countries), light and dark theme, interface in 6 languages

PRIVACY
The subtitle text and the words you save are sent to our server (and to Google Translate) to be translated and stored. We never sell your data. You can delete your account and all your data at any time. Full policy: https://cinevocab.up.railway.app/privacy
